About Pinball Caddy

Notes were sourced from pinballrulesheets.com. You can create your own notes as well and toggle as necessary. Notes from pinballrulesheets were extracted using AI and may not be accurate.

Personal Notes & Toggles: Tap any machine to view its Quick-Look guide, tap Edit / Notes to customize any field or add personal tournament notes, and use the toggle pill on edited fields to switch between your notes and the original rulesheet.

Code Releases & Rulesheet Updates: Tap Code Releases in the bottom bar to browse recent manufacturer software updates (with release notes and official links) and new or updated rulesheets from pinballrulesheets.com (showing whether each rulesheet has been added to Pinball Caddy or is pending import). New releases also appear in a dismissible 14-day alert banner at the top of the library.
